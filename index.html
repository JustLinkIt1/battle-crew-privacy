Battle Crew — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Battle Crew is a single-player real-time strategy game published by Nathan Joppich. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information. The short version: we run no server and receive nothing about you. Your progress is saved on your own device, and the only information that ever leaves it is your Google Play Games player ID — and only if you choose to sign in for achievements and leaderboards.

Information we collect

Battle Crew does not sell or share your information, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or third-party tracking. We do not operate a server and we receive nothing from the game.

The one exception is optional: if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services, Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID so that achievements and leaderboard scores can be attached to your gamer profile. See the section below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress so you can continue a campaign later. This is written to your device's local browser storage inside the app and never leaves your device. It contains only game state, such as:

  • campaign progress and which regions you hold
  • unlocked commanders and upgrades
  • your best score and unlocked achievements

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android's app settings, permanently deletes this data. We never receive a copy of it and cannot retrieve or restore it.

Network access

The Android app declares the INTERNET permission because it is built on a web view, but all game content — artwork, 3D models and code — is bundled inside the app and loaded from local storage. The game is fully playable offline and contacts no server we operate.

Google Play Games Services

Battle Crew uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and leaderboards. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it — every mode, mission and feature is available whether you sign in or not.

If you do sign in, Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID. The game uses it only to record achievements you unlock and scores you set, so they appear on your Play Games profile. We do not receive your name, email address or Google account details, we do not combine this with anything else, and we do not share it with anyone.

Play Games Services is operated by Google, and your use of it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can review or delete your Play Games data, and disconnect the game, from the Play Games app under Settings, or at your Google Account.

Children

Battle Crew is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google Play Games Services applies its own age restrictions to sign-in.

Your rights

We hold no database and no account of yours, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct or export. Your campaign save lives on your own device; uninstalling the app deletes it. If you signed in to Play Games Services, that data is held by Google under your own Google Account, and you can review or delete it there at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
